## What the existing mesh checks prove

Depending on the script and asset, they can show file presence, vertex/face counts, bounding boxes, selected slice radii, watertightness, volume when watertight, duplicate vertices, and degenerate triangles. These are useful geometry diagnostics.

They do not prove assembly constraints, joint retention, motion range, interference throughout motion, fastener access, wire routing, actual component envelopes, manufacturing tolerances, material/process suitability, mass-property linkage, loads, structural margins, maintainability, assembly sequence, or successful physical manufacture.
